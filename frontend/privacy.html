SocialSpork

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

SocialSpork ("SocialSpork", "we", "us") provides an analytics dashboard for restaurant and retail brands at app.socialspork.com. It brings together a brand's own data from food delivery platforms (such as Zomato and Swiggy) and from Google Business Profile, so the brand can track ratings, reviews, sales, stock-outs, payouts and listing performance in one place. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Who this policy covers

  • Customers: the businesses that sign up for SocialSpork, and the people they invite to use the dashboard.
  • Their end customers: people who order from or review our customers' outlets, whose information appears in data the platforms provide to the business.

2. Information we collect

  • Account information: name, email address, role and the brands and outlets a user can see.
  • Delivery platform data, accessed with the business's permission: outlet details, orders and sales, menu and stock status, ratings, reviews, complaints, payouts and ad spend.
  • Google Business Profile data, when a business connects its Google account (see section 4).
  • Customer contact numbers: only where the end customer chose, on the delivery platform, to share their number with the restaurant. We store these encrypted, show them masked by default, and log every time a number is viewed.
  • Usage information: basic logs of sign-ins and actions in the dashboard, used for security and support.

3. How we use information

  • To show each business its own data and insights in the dashboard, and to send the alerts it asks for (for example by email or WhatsApp).
  • To keep the service secure, fix problems and provide support.

We do not sell personal information, do not use it for advertising, and do not share one business's data with another business.

4. Google user data

When a business clicks Connect with Google, we ask for access to Google Business Profile (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/business.manage) and the email address of the Google account.

  • What we access: the business locations that Google account manages, their profile details (name, address, category, hours, attributes), customer reviews and review replies, and performance metrics (such as profile views, searches, calls and direction requests).
  • How we use it: only to show this information to that business in its SocialSpork dashboard, to compute insights for it, and to alert it about changes (for example a new low-rated review or a listing problem). We read data; we do not post, edit or delete anything on Google unless the business explicitly asks us to from the dashboard.
  • Sharing: Google user data is shown only to the business that connected it and the users it invites. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or transfer it to third parties, except as needed to run the service (our hosting provider), to comply with law, or as part of a merger or acquisition with notice to you.
  • Humans: our staff do not read Google user data except with the business's permission (for example to resolve a support request), for security, or to comply with law.
  • AI models: we do not use Google user data to train generalised AI or machine-learning models.
  • Storage and security: Google access tokens are encrypted at rest. Data is stored on Amazon Web Services in India (Mumbai) and sent only over encrypted connections.
  • Removing access: a business can disconnect Google at any time from Brands & Outlets in the dashboard, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After disconnecting, we stop fetching new data. To also delete stored Google data, email us (section 7) and we will delete it within 30 days.

SocialSpork's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Service providers

We use a small number of providers to run SocialSpork: Amazon Web Services (hosting and database), Cloudflare (network security), and email and WhatsApp messaging providers for alerts the business has set up. They process data only on our instructions.

6. Retention

We keep a business's data for as long as its account is active, so that it can see history and trends. When a business closes its account or asks us to delete its data, we delete it within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep it longer.

7. Your choices and contact

To access, correct or delete information, disconnect a platform, or ask any question about this policy, email consult@socialspork.com. End customers who want their information removed can contact the restaurant or email us, and we will help.

8. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above, and tell customers of significant changes by email or in the dashboard.
