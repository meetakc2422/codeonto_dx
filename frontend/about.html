One dashboard for a restaurant brand's delivery platforms and Google listings

Codeonto helps restaurant and retail brands run their outlets on Zomato, Swiggy and Google from one place. Brand owners and their teams sign in at compass.codeonto.com to see, for every outlet:

  • Ratings and reviews from Zomato, Swiggy and Google, with alerts for new low ratings and a place to reply.
  • Sales and orders, item by item.
  • Stock-outs: which dishes are off the menu, where, and for how long.
  • Store uptime: when an outlet went offline during opening hours.
  • Payouts and reconciliation: whether platform payouts match the orders.
  • Google Business Profile performance: profile views, searches, calls and direction requests, and listing health.

How Codeonto uses Google

A brand can click Connect with Google to link the Google account that manages its Business Profile listings. Codeonto then reads, for those listings only, the location details, customer reviews and performance metrics, and shows them in that brand's own dashboard. Codeonto does not post, edit or delete anything on Google unless the brand asks it to from the dashboard, does not sell or share Google data, and does not use it for advertising or to train AI models. A brand can disconnect at any time.

Full details are in our Privacy Policy, including how Codeonto follows the Google API Services User Data Policy and its Limited Use requirements.

Who it is for

Restaurant groups, cloud kitchens and QSR chains that sell through food delivery apps and want one view of all their outlets. Access is by invitation: Codeonto sets up each brand's account and its outlets.
